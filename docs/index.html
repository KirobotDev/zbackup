ZBackup is a free and open source backup solution with built-in version control, designed to protect everything from small personal files to very large projects with speed and efficiency.

ZBackup is lightning fast and provides ZIP backups, restore, and cross-platform support.

Why ZBackup?

Lightning Fast

ZIP-based backups created instantly. No bloated configurations, just fast and reliable results.

Simple CLI

Interactive terminal interface. Create, restore, list, and delete backups with simple commands.

Cross-Platform

Works on both Windows and Linux. ZBackup adapts to your operating system automatically.

Open Source

MIT licensed. Free to use, modify, and distribute. Your data stays yours, always.
